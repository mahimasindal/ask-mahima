Implosive Silence

There are many voices you hear since you are born but the one that sticks with you is the one that makes up a world nobody can ever see or will ever have access to. When you grow from a toddler to a child to a teenager to an adult, this voice grows with you. Basic reason that I justify for the growth of this voice is the fact that children blurt out all kinds of stuff, good bad weird directly without filters, but as we grow these filters start growing. Each time we are told not to say something to put a finger on our mouth, this very voice becomes loud and before we even realise we discover this inner world where we are the only one talking and listening at the same time. But I remember even as a child, I had so many thoughts I thought and never told anyone while walking one day going to school. It was a long straight road covered with trees closed my eyes, and in my mind, thought that I am not even in this world and just existing somewhere else only when I grew up realised those were some strange thoughts for a child to have, but then I also wondered there must be other people who will have similar thoughts. There is no way to know this, but one of the most important thing is this voice suddenly start taking control of you. In the world of people, talking to each other, telling each other things that doesn’t even matter, for you somewhere, your voice becomes everything. You have it even in the silence of the trees and chirping of the birds. You listen to it and start believing everything it tells you. The incidents, the core of your life is nothing but a narrator lying deep somewhere in your head, dictating random thoughts and events, making your life’s documentary. The more you realise less and less people understand and feel the way you do your physical voice declines. You speak less and implosive silence, Originates like a little swirl of wind flickering a few leaves, but the flickering increases day by day the storm inside the unsettling rage towards how you were born and simply left to endure. Some questions are always open in your head or heart, or whatever is the origin of the silence. Pain that formed for years, now lies in this territory of silence. We think thoughts and leave them there. Nothing is ever resolved because this life it was there to do, but we know no question of ours will ever get resolved. The silence is filling with the questions and then the pain, but you have grown quite you will try to behave. Yes just try most of the time you will be successful most of the days you will get up, get dressed, go to work and talk to people, not what you actually want to talk about, but whatever you must talk about All these days, the silence inside you will grow. You will be with the group of people, laughing and talking, but you will realise the presence of the silence it’s growing and growing until one day everything becomes black. The chirping of the bird stop people stop talking the food stops tasting. You lie in the emptiness of your 2BHK staring at the empty dirty ceiling, not knowing what to do or how to feel nothing is actually changed, but the silence or rather the voice of the silence couldn’t take it anymore, it has reached its limit of infinity Because yes, there are limits to infinity as well, and the infinity is broken. The silence implodes not outside, not like a crying child, not like the thunderstorm but inside your body. You are the only one that can listen to it. You feel like as there is no one to share this silence The intensity of it to you multiplies even more you burst not outside but within your own self. everything is happening inside you and now parts of your thoughts are leaking inside you and you can’t help it in conversations and with laughing people and with people who tries to love you or hate you because the silence imploded. Picture the atom bomb, exploding white fumes coming out, but you contain silence, and it’s transferring you are the medium and you start shivering with the weight of the infinity that you carry leaking inside you, but you have to Shout in the middle of the night for how else will the voice of silence echo into the actual existence and leave the ruins of your soul.


Becoming Still

Now you want to go here and there and search for something you know you will find only by being still. But it’s not possible for you to be still. You are anxious and majorly you are the protagonist if you become still so easy how will the story go on. You need to struggle and find yourself amidst the terror of existence that you don’t want to even be here and be anywhere. You want to run away, you have no place to call home. It’s one big nomadic feeling in your heart that you don’t belong but it’s good to not belong after all this time you are not ordinary. You cannot be settled down because you feel like everything is on fire and you are in the house watching letting it burn because you like the warmth of something. The fumes are rising and you are loosing all consciousness and you can hear voices and see blurry faces but you can’t separate the reality and imagination or may be they have become one after all. So you finally fall down and become still.
